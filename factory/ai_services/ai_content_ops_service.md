# AI Content Operations Service

## Offer
A productized service for small online businesses that need structured content operations without hiring a full-time content coordinator.

## Starter deliverables
- 10 product-description rewrites
- 10 SEO title/meta-description pairs
- 5 customer-support response templates
- 1 FAQ cleanup pass
- 1 content consistency report

## Standard workflow
1. Client supplies source product/service information.
2. Remove or mask unnecessary sensitive information.
3. Build a source-of-truth fact sheet.
4. Draft content from supplied facts only.
5. Run a consistency and unsupported-claim check.
6. Deliver editable files plus a change log.

## Quality rules
- Never invent specifications, certifications, testimonials, guarantees, or performance claims.
- Flag missing facts rather than guessing.
- Keep regulated medical, legal, and financial claims out of scope unless appropriately reviewed.
- Preserve client brand terms and required disclaimers.

## Packaging tests
- Starter: narrow batch for one catalog/category.
- Growth: recurring monthly content-ops batch.
- Custom: larger catalogs with scoped human review.

## Machine-compatible intake fields
business_name, website, product_source_files, brand_voice, prohibited_claims, required_terms, target_channels, output_format, deadline
